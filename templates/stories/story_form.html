GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take?

Generation time depends on the text and illustration providers configured for this app.

Can I save or print the story?

Yes. Once a story finishes generating, use the PDF download button to keep or print it.

Are stories suitable for children?

Stories are designed for young readers, but an adult should review generated content before sharing.

Can I include more than one child?

This version creates a story around one main character at a time.

Can I add a reference photo?

Photo uploads are not enabled in this version. Add a written appearance description to guide illustrations.

Is story creation free?

There is no checkout in this version. Create a story and download the completed PDF from the app.
